Method

Recency is whole days from the last purchase to midnight after the analysis date. Frequency counts unique invoices. Monetary is the sum of quantity × unit price.

Each dimension is ranked into a 1–5 score (5 is best) using average-rank percentiles. The RFM score weights recency 15%, frequency 28% and monetary 57%, scaled to 100. Segments are assigned from the R and F scores only, so high spenders who have lapsed still show up as at risk.
